# Jay Technology Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The Jay Technology Sales Analysis project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive view of business performance across five analytical sections: Executive, Sales, Products, Returns, and Insights.

The dashboard brings together key business performance indicators, sales analysis, product performance, return analysis, and actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Technology retailers need to monitor overall performance, understand sales trends, evaluate product contributions, and track product returns. Analysing these areas separately can make it difficult to understand the relationship between sales performance, product demand, and returns.

This project addresses these challenges through a multi-page Power BI dashboard that organises business information into a clear and interactive reporting solution.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance through executive-level KPIs.
- Analyse sales performance across the available business dimensions.
- Evaluate product sales and identify differences in product performance.
- Examine product returns and their implications for business performance.
- Present key findings and recommendations to support decision-making.
- Provide an interactive reporting experience for exploring business data.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development and interactive data visualisation.
- **DAX:** Measures and calculations for business performance analysis.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.
- **Data Modelling:** Structuring relationships between relevant data tables.
- **Business Intelligence:** KPI monitoring, performance evaluation, and insight development.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard

The Executive page provides a high-level overview of business performance through key performance indicators and summary visualisations.

It helps stakeholders quickly assess the overall state of the business before exploring more detailed sales and product information.

**Analytical focus:**
- Executive-level performance monitoring.
- Summary of key business metrics.
- High-level performance comparisons.
- Quick identification of areas requiring further investigation.

### 2. Sales Analysis

The Sales page focuses on understanding sales performance across the available reporting periods and business dimensions.

It enables users to explore sales patterns and compare performance using the information provided in the dashboard.

**Analytical focus:**
- Sales performance and trends.
- Comparisons across available sales dimensions.
- Identification of changes in sales performance.
- Exploration of sales patterns over time.

### 3. Product Analysis

The Products page examines product-level performance to help users understand how individual products or product categories contribute to business results.

**Analytical focus:**
- Product sales performance.
- Comparison of product contributions.
- Identification of differences across products.
- Evaluation of product performance patterns.

### 4. Returns Analysis

The Returns page focuses on returned products and return-related performance indicators.

Return analysis is important because product returns can affect net sales, customer experience, inventory management, and operational efficiency.

**Analytical focus:**
- Monitoring return-related metrics.
- Examining available return patterns.
- Comparing returns across relevant product or business dimensions.
- Identifying areas that may require further investigation.

### 5. Insights & Recommendations

The Insights page brings together important observations from the analysis and presents recommendations intended to support business decision-making.

Rather than simply displaying figures, this section focuses on interpreting the information and explaining its potential business relevance.

**Analytical focus:**
- Summarising important findings from the dashboard.
- Highlighting patterns identified through the analysis.
- Connecting observations to potential business implications.
- Presenting recommendations supported by the available data.

## 🔍 Analytical Workflow

The project follows a structured business intelligence workflow:

1. **Data Understanding:** Review the available data and identify the fields relevant to sales, products, and returns.
2. **Data Preparation:** Prepare the data for analysis and address relevant data quality issues.
3. **Data Transformation:** Organise and transform the data into a suitable analytical structure.
4. **Data Modelling:** Establish relationships needed to analyse the relevant business dimensions.
5. **Measure Development:** Use appropriate calculations to support KPI reporting and performance analysis.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Build interactive pages for executive reporting, sales, products, returns, and insights.
7. **Insight Communication:** Present findings and recommendations in a format that supports business understanding.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of important business activities and can help stakeholders:

- Monitor business performance more efficiently.
- Understand sales performance and product contributions.
- Identify product and return patterns that deserve attention.
- Explore potential areas for operational improvement.
- Communicate business findings through a structured reporting interface.
- Support decisions with evidence from the available data.

## ⚠️ Limitations

The analysis depends on the quality, completeness, and reporting period of the underlying dataset. The interpretation of sales and returns also depends on how the relevant metrics are defined and calculated.

Observed patterns can identify areas for further investigation, but they do not independently establish the causes of changes in sales or product returns.

## 🏁 Conclusion

The Jay Technology Sales Analysis project demonstrates how Power BI can transform business data into a structured, interactive reporting solution.

By combining executive reporting, sales analysis, product performance, returns analysis, and business insights in one dashboard, the project provides a comprehensive framework for exploring business performance and supporting data-informed decisions.
